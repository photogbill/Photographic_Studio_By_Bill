# What is in the next update

<!--

  Start each line with "- ". push.bat sends these lines as the comment; Release.bat then moves them into

- New: Paint > Brush > Edit brush: every setting of a brush with a live preview stroke that repaints as you move a slider (tip, opacity and flow, which pen inputs drive size, opacity and flow with a curve for each, texture and colour). Save keeps it for next time; Save as new brush makes your own copy; Back to how it came undoes everything since the window opened.
- New: Paint brushes can have paper grain (cold-press, rough charcoal, fine, canvas weave, laid paper, or any picture): a light touch catches only the paper's ridges and pressing harder fills the valleys, and the grain stays put on the paper as you paint.
- New: Paint brushes can have a second tip that breaks up the dab (as Photoshop's Dual Brush), a count of dabs at each spot for sprays, and colour jitter in hue, saturation and value per dab or per stroke. New brushes use them: Charcoal (rough paper), Soft pastel, Dry brush (canvas), Spray and Leaves (colour jitter).
- New: Paint has seven new brush engines: Spray (particles), Chalk (crumbly dabs that fill with pressure), Sketch (web lines back to earlier parts of the stroke), Hatching (parallel ink lines, cross-hatched when you press hard), Shape (draw an outline and it fills), Clone (Ctrl+click where to copy from) and Tangent normal (paints normal maps for games from the pen's tilt).
- New: Paint imports other programs' brushes: Photoshop .abr, Clip Studio .sut and .sutg, Krita .kpp, and GIMP .gbr and .gih (Brush tab > Import brushes). The tips come across; most of the other programs' settings do not, so set the dynamics in Edit brush. Imported brushes are kept for next time.
- New: Paint comes with 135 brushes, each based on a real tool and medium and saying what it is best for and weak at: graphite grades 6H to 8B, silverpoint, dip pens and technical pens, hog and sable brushes, knives, fan, rigger and mop, watercolour and gouache, charcoal, pastel, conté and sanguine, airbrushes, blenders, texture brushes, East Asian ink brushes and texture strokes. The brush list has categories and a search box.
- New: Paint has real watercolour: on its own layer, washes flow and dry by themselves, with darker edges, blooms where water meets damp paint, pigment settling into the paper's grain, and lifting with a clean wet brush. New brushes: Wet wash, Wet round, Wet-in-wet bloom, Granulating wash, Lifting brush and Wet dry brush.
- New: Paint has thick paint (impasto): brushes can lay paint with thickness, knives and smudges push it into ridges, and Layers > Paint relief shows it under a light you can move. Exports can include the relief, and .ora files keep it.
- New: Paint has a bristle engine: every bristle carries its own paint, picks up colour from the canvas and runs dry, so strokes streak and break up like real oil, acrylic and gouache. New brushes: Oil bristle, Thin oil, Acrylic flat, Gouache bristle, Impasto bristle, Impasto round and Impasto palette knife.
- New: Paint > Select: rectangle, ellipse, lasso, polygon, magic wand and colour range, added, subtracted or intersected; grow, shrink, feather, invert and select from a layer. Brushes, fill, gradients, filters and transforms stay inside the selection. Cut, copy, paste (as a new layer), delete and fill.
- New: Paint transforms (move, scale, rotate, skew, flip) always work from the pixels kept at the start, so a layer moved ten times is as sharp as one moved once; and Liquify pushes paint around with a brush.
- New: Paint layers can have masks (paint on the mask: black hides, white shows), clipping to the layer below, and adjustment layers (curves, levels, hue/saturation, brightness/contrast, colour balance, black and white, invert, posterize) that change what is below without touching the paint. Filters: blur, sharpen, noise, motion blur and smooth. All kept in .ora files.
- New: Paint opens and saves Photoshop .psd files with their layers, groups, masks, clipping, opacity and blend modes, and opens Krita .kra paintings (layers and groups; anything it cannot bring across is said).
- New: Paint transforms have handles on the canvas: Free (drag inside to move, a corner to scale, outside to rotate), Perspective (drag the corners), Warp and Mesh (drag the grid), and Cage (place points around a shape, then drag them). Enter keeps it, Esc undoes it.
- New: Paint has filter layers (a blur or other filter on everything below, changeable later), fill layers (a colour you can change any time), file layers (a picture file linked as a layer, with Reload), the Studio's Natural curve as an adjustment layer, the Studio's Clean as a filter, and Subject select (click a figure or object and the Studio's SAM model outlines it).
- New: Paint paints in any working colour space: sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto, or a linear floating-point HDR canvas with a view exposure control; convert a painting between them; and soft proof against any printer profile (CMYK included), with a mark on colours the paper cannot hold.
- New: Paint opens and exports OpenEXR (floating point, values above white kept, for 3D and games), opens Photoshop .psb and ZIP-compressed .psd files, and keeps the colour profile in .psd files both ways.
